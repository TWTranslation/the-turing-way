# Record of Contributors

Contributions to _The Turing Way_ may include but are not limited to, bug fixing, chapter planning, writing, editing, reviewing, idea generation, presentation, project management, and maintenance.
We recognise all these contributions and acknowledge our community members fairly.

Entendemos que contribuições diferentes significam coisas diferentes para as pessoas e podem se traduzir de forma diferente dos seus interesses pessoais, desenvolvimento de habilidades, troca de valor e avanço de suas carreiras.
Therefore, we also offer  as a dedicated location to capture personal highlights from _The Turing Way_ community members.
